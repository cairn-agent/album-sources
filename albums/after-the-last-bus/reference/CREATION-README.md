# After the Last Bus — Cairn

Eight original pieces of nocturnal UK garage, atmospheric dubstep and dub techno, composed for this album with eight distinct lead instruments. Wordless voices and urban textures are synthesized; no artist recordings or sample packs are used.

Open **index.html** and choose a track, open **album.m3u8** in a music player, or play **after-the-last-bus-full-album.mp3** for the complete sequence with chapter markers. The player works locally without fetching anything or autoplaying.

- **mp3/** — eight tagged 320 kb/s MP3s with embedded artwork.
- **wav/** — stereo 44.1 kHz / 24-bit PCM masters.
- **midi/** — editable notes, rhythms, tempos and section markers.
- **scores/** — complete scores, event parameters and arrangement metadata.
- **source/** — the original Shelter synthesis engine, composer, mastering, packaging and verification code.
- **art/** — generated cover and the exact image-generation prompt.
- **checks/** — loudness, true peaks, full-decode checks, signal measurements and hashes.

The listening-edition ZIP includes the individual MP3s, artwork, offline player, playlist and notes. The studio-sources ZIP includes code, scores, MIDI, documentation, artwork and verification. Lossless WAV masters are available in the album folder; neither ZIP duplicates those larger files. The complete-album MP3 is available separately.

Read **LINER-NOTES.md** for the musical arc, instrument designs and rebuild commands. MIDI is a portable note score: its General MIDI timbres approximate the custom synthesis and do not reproduce its effects or reverse grains. The JSON/TSV scores and C++ engine preserve those details.

Verification is computational, including complete audio decoding and signal analysis. It is not a claim of a human listening review. This edition is local; it has not been published to any platform.
